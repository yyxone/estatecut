# Review an edit decision list

Read the transcript and the relevant source intervals before proposing edits.
Preserve meaningful speech and its timing. An ASR gap is not proof of silence.
Keep ranges are source-time intervals. Quote the evidence for every proposed cut.
Leave uncertain intervals intact and ask for review of that specific interval.
Do not set approval locks on behalf of a human or claim that a generated plan is approved.
After a reviewer approves transcript, cuts and subtitles, use the corresponding CLI locks.
Synthetic smoke-test locks do not authorize any real-media edit.
